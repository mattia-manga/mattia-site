01 / About

Process before tools.

My path combines ERP consulting, business process knowledge and Quality Assurance activities. I'm interested in understanding how a process actually works, what information it uses and where inefficiencies, redundancies or critical points arise.

The goal is to help define operating models that are simpler, more controllable and more effective, while keeping process, data and information system consistent with one another.

04 / Approach

Understand. Structure. Improve.

01

Observe the real process

Start from what actually happens operationally, not from what should happen on paper.

02

Make critical points visible

Identify redundant steps, error risks, missing information and unclear responsibilities.

03

Choose the right tool

Use technology and management systems only when they produce a real benefit for the process.
